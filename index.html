Addicted Music was founded in 2025 with one mission: to discover, develop, and amplify the rawest talent coming out of Port Harcourt and beyond. We are not just a label — we are a movement.

From the streets to the charts, our artists deliver unfiltered storytelling that moves culture. Whether it's Afrosounds, Hip-Hop, or the space in between, Addicted Music is the sound of a new generation.

Mosidon

Afrosounds / Hip-Hop

Mosidon is the flagship artist of Addicted Music — a voice that carries the grit and ambition of Port Harcourt. With a catalog that spans from high-energy street anthems to introspective storytelling, he represents everything the label stands for.

From the breakout "MMD (Money Must Dey)" to the full-length project "Trap in PHC", Mosidon continues to push boundaries and define what it means to be an artist from the Niger Delta.
